## Reviewing a Codex PR

When asked to review a PR, follow the "Reviewing" section of `docs/planning/agent-workflow.md`: read the real diff and the code around it against the linked issue's acceptance criteria (not the PR description), check protected data, migrations, security, accessibility and scope creep, and post one comment that starts with **Approve**, **Request changes** (file and line) or **Escalate** (link a decision issue). Do not merge. On `risk:high` tickets, answer the plan posted on the issue first with "proceed" or constraints.
